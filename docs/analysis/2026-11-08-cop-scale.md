# COP scale for UA_eff (#105): formula vs live sensor vs heat-pump counters

2026-11-08 · script `scripts/analyze_cop_scale.py` · data: HA long-term hourly statistics 2026-03-24 .. 2026-11-07

## Question

The UA_eff calibrator converts heating electricity into delivered heat with the config COP formula
`min(0.45 * Carnot(heating curve), a + b*T)` with `a=2.5, b=0.07` (≈ 3.1 at 8 °C). The live Kermi
`cop_heating_live` sensor reads ≈ 5. UA scales linearly with COP, so this was flagged as an open
blocker for #105 (`memory/project_ua_eff_null_cause_and_path.md`: "UA uncertain up to ×1.75").

## Method

The Kermi controller publishes cumulative counters for delivered heat (heating + DHW,
`lifetime_heat`) and for electricity split into heating and DHW. `lifetime_electricity` equals
`electricity_heating + electricity_dhw` to the 0.01 kWh on every day, so the split is complete.
Per complete local day (≥ 22 valid hours) we fit `heat = COP_h·el_heat + COP_d·el_dhw` (no intercept),
then compute an energy-weighted heating COP per outdoor-temperature bin (days with ≥ 2 kWh heating
electricity), with 95 % day-bootstrap intervals. Outdoor temperature: `sensor.outdoor_netatmo_temperature`
(the Kermi outdoor average used live has no long-term statistics). UA: daily heat balance
`q = UA·24·(T_in − T_out)` (± PV-production term as solar proxy), T_in = area-weighted Netatmo rooms.

## Findings

1. **The formula is ≈ 35 % too low** across every bin with data (2.9 vs 4.2 at 6 °C, 3.2 vs 4.8 at 10 °C).
   The binding term is the linear `2.5 + 0.07·T`; implied Carnot efficiency ≈ 0.27–0.30, unrealistic
   for a modern inverter heat pump. The counters imply η ≈ 0.40–0.48, the normal range.
2. **The live sensor agrees with the counters** within 0.05–0.33 (sensor slightly optimistic in the
   colder bins). Note this is not an independent check: both come from the same controller, and the
   heat counter is estimated by the controller, not a calibrated heat meter. The physical plausibility
   (η ≈ 0.4–0.48) is the independent argument.
3. **UA with the formula is ≈ 35 % too low** (84 vs 128–133 W/K), but for the *electricity forecast*
   the scale mostly cancels: calibration (`physics.py:810`, `:876`) and prediction (`:561`, `:610`) both
   use the formula, so `el = UA·ΔT / COP` comes back right if the COP is off by a constant factor.
   What does not cancel is the **shape over temperature**: counter/formula ratio is 1.32 at 3 °C,
   1.43 at 6 °C, 1.52 at 10 °C, 1.53 at 14 °C. With UA fitted on mild days, the formula therefore
   under-forecasts heating electricity on cold days by roughly 10–15 % (more below the data range).
   UA as a physical number (c_building/τ, sanity window) is simply wrong with the formula, and any
   mix (live sensor in prediction, formula in calibration) would be inconsistent by the full ~1.5×.
   (The 2026-10-17 "October ≈ 28 W/K" figure came from a different, smaller fit and is superseded.)
4. **Refit of the linear term** on heating days (season, n=26, el-weighted): `a = 3.53, b = 0.147`
   (residual sd 0.37); all days (n=66): `a = 3.32, b = 0.144` (sd 0.46). With the existing Carnot
   cap the refitted formula hands over to Carnot·0.45 below ≈ 5 °C.
5. **Cold days are unmeasured.** Only 3 days below 4 °C, none below 2.6 °C. Anything colder is extrapolation.

## Recommendation for #105

- **COP scale is no longer a blocker for #105**: it is resolved (formula ≈ 1.5× low, sensor ≈ counters),
  and for the electricity forecast only the temperature shape matters.
- Calibrate UA from **delivered heat**, not from electricity × assumed COP: `heat − COP_d·el_dhw`
  per day from the counters (or hourly `el_heat × cop_heating_live`). Then prediction must use a COP
  on the same scale: the refitted formula (`a ≈ 3.5, b ≈ 0.15`, Carnot cap unchanged) for future hours.
  Calibration and prediction must change together; changing only one side is worse than today.
- The minimal alternative (keep formula-based calibration, only refit `a`/`b`) fixes the shape error
  and the UA scale at once with one config change. Either way it is a BEHAVIOUR change that ships with
  or after #105, after the A+B re-baseline holds.
- Re-check the cold bins after the first sustained sub-zero week.
- Expected UA floor/ceiling: the 128–142 W/K range sits well inside the 50–500 W/K sanity window.
